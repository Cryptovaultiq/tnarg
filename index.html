How to Check Your Refund Status

Follow these steps to track your federal tax refund securely

1

Prepare your return information (SSN, filing status, refund amount)

2

Click "Check Refund Status" and complete the secure form

3

Enter your details in the secure verification portal

4

View your current status and estimated timeline

Important: The IRS updates refund status information once per day, usually overnight. Check back every 24 hours for the most current information.

Grant Scam Warning

Be cautious of emails, calls, or websites claiming you've been "selected" for a grant or asking for payment to access grant information. The federal government does not charge fees to apply for grants or randomly award grants to individuals.
